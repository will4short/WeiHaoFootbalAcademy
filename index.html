About Wei Hao Football Academy

At Wei Hao Football Academy, we help children and youth develop football skills, confidence, teamwork, discipline, and communication.

Our Kick & Speak™ approach combines professional football training with English communication in a fun and encouraging environment.

We believe every child can grow through sport — both on and off the field.

Programs

  • Little Kickers (Ages 4–6)
  • Junior Academy (Ages 7–10)
  • Youth Development (Ages 11–14)
  • Elite Training Squad (Ages 15–18)
