Questions from families

Good to know before you come.

Still unsure? Send us a message and we will help you choose the most suitable starting point.

Does my child need football experience?+

No. Players are supported at their current level, from a first experience with the ball to more advanced development.

How much English does my child need?+

No specific level is required. Instructions are connected to movement and demonstrated clearly so players can learn naturally through play.

What should my child wear?+

Comfortable sportswear and safe footwear suitable for the confirmed surface. Bring water and weather-appropriate clothing.

Can parents stay during the trial?+

Parent and guardian arrangements will be explained with the confirmed venue and age group before the session.

What happens in bad weather?+

The academy will communicate any weather-related change or cancellation directly using the contact details provided at booking.

Are locations, times and fees final?+

Not yet. The academy is in pre-launch planning. Full details will be confirmed in writing before a place or payment is accepted.

Our pre-launch commitments

Care, clarity and respect from day one.

These summaries explain how we intend to operate while final venue-specific procedures and enrollment terms are being prepared.

01Safeguarding+

The welfare of every child comes first. Training will use age-appropriate coaching, respectful conduct and clear boundaries. A parent or guardian will provide consent for participation, photography and the handling of relevant information. Safeguarding concerns can be reported privately to the academy by email, telephone or WhatsApp and will be taken seriously, documented appropriately and referred to the relevant authority when required.

02Bookings & cancellations+

Trial places are not confirmed until the academy sends written confirmation. Families should give as much notice as possible if they cannot attend. If the academy cancels a confirmed session, families will be offered a rescheduled session or an appropriate credit or refund where payment was made. Final fees, notice periods and refund terms will be shown before any paid enrollment is accepted.

03Privacy+

Kick & Speak Football Academy collects the parent or guardian contact details, player age, preferred city, program interest and message that you voluntarily provide. We use this information in Taiwan to answer enquiries, plan trial groups and communicate about academy services. It may be handled by authorized academy personnel and the email, telephone or messaging providers you choose to use. We do not sell personal data. We retain it only while reasonably needed for these purposes or a legal obligation. Providing information is optional, but without the required contact and player details we may be unable to respond or recommend a suitable group. Please do not send sensitive medical or identification information through this website.

You may ask to review or receive a copy of your data, request a correction, ask us to stop using it, or request deletion where legally available. Contact info@weihaoacademy.com.
